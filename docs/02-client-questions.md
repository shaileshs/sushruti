# Questions for Dr. Shah

We need answers to these four to keep building. Each one has our suggestion and why — reply with a yes, or tell us what you'd rather have.

1. **Your case-taking form.** Please send your current case-taking form — paper, Word file, or a photo of it — so our visit form matches what you already use.
   *Suggested: send it as-is, even rough — a photo of a handwritten form is enough. We'll match it exactly and adjust later if you send a cleaner version. Waiting for a polished version only slows us down.*

2. **Remedy, potency, dose per visit.** You described treatment as a sequence: give a remedy, watch the response, then decide the next one. Should we record the remedy, potency, and dose at each visit from the start, or add this in a later update?
   *Suggested: record it from the start. If we add it later, any patients seen before then have no record of what was given — you'd have to reconstruct it from memory. Capturing it now costs little and protects that history.*

3. **Photos.** Do you want lesion photos kept with each visit, so you can compare across visits? Or kept with the patient only, not tied to one visit?
   *Suggested: keep them with each visit. That's what lets you see a lesion change over time, visit to visit — which is the whole point of the trend view. Photos on the patient alone would lose that timeline.*

4. **More than one lesion.** Does a single patient ever have more than one lesion you track separately?
   *Suggested: assume one lesion per patient for now, since that's the common case. If you often see more than one, tell us — it's easier for us to build for that from the start than to change it after we've launched.*
